Certificate renewals

Every renewal attempt on every node — what ran, when, and the exact error when it failed. The nightly satom-cert-renew timer used to leave its result only in journalctl, so a silent failure was invisible until the expiry alert fired. The alert e-mail tells you something is wrong; this page tells you what.
