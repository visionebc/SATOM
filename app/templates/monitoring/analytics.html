Collection cadence

A probe fires only when its own interval has elapsed and the sweep happens to run, so its real cadence is tick × ceil(interval ÷ tick). A five-minute probe under a three-minute sweep is a six-minute probe — and its own row still says five. Any probe whose interval is not a multiple of the tick is flagged below.
