Sentinel

Correlates attack signatures, HTTP outcomes, appliance counters, VM and hypervisor metrics and vulnerability intelligence into one incident — and explains every number it shows.

{# ── the sweep picker ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────── A sweep is fleet-wide, and the button used to start one over every device with no way to say otherwise and no way to watch it. Two separate defects were behind that, and the modal only fixes the smaller one: the work now goes to the JOB ledger, so it survives this page, reports per device and can be stopped. Only FortiWeb appliances are listed, and that is not an omission. What a sweep ingests is the FortiWeb attack log; a FortiADC has none, so offering one here would be offering a selection that can only ever return nothing. Ineligible devices (maintenance, retired `.invalid` hosts) ARE listed, with the reason and unselectable: the pipeline skips them deliberately, and a device missing from the list looks like a device that does not exist. #}
Run a sweep

A sweep reads each appliance's attack log, normalises and de-duplicates it, routes new events to incidents, re-scores every live incident and expires actions whose TTL has passed. It runs as a job: this page does not wait for it, progress appears in the Jobs dock, and it can be stopped between devices. Selecting nothing sweeps every eligible device.
