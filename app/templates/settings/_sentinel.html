These live outside this form because they are not single values. Each one is a real prerequisite, and each degrades honestly rather than silently when it is missing.

{{ _('Prerequisite') }}{{ _('Why, and what happens without it') }}
Topology map Binds each appliance to its VM id and hypervisor node. Without it the vm and host layers report unknown on every incident for that device — never “no impact”.
Hypervisor target
(Settings → Hypervisors)		 The Proxmox/ESXi credentials the infra collector reads through. Absent, that collector fails loudly per target rather than publishing zeros.
Collection targets Sentinel adds two collectors — http_status (response classes from the traffic log) and infra. Their cadence is edited on the same page as every other collector, on purpose: a second collection settings page is how two cadence models drift apart.
Scheduled actions sentinel_sweep every 3 minutes, sentinel_baseline nightly, sentinel_vuln_sync daily. Without the sweep nothing is detected, and the console says “never run” rather than showing an empty, healthy-looking list.
Local model host Only for the narrative. If it is unreachable the incident keeps its deterministic score, its evidence and its policy decision — it loses the prose.
CVE mirror content Sync (needs the outbound switch above) or enter CVEs by hand under Context. An empty mirror means CVE factors simply do not apply; it never fabricates one.
